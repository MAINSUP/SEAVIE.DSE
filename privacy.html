Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 30, 2026  |  Effective Date: August 30, 2026  |  Developer: DSE — Data Science Enterprise

This Privacy Policy describes how SeaVie ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials through Supabase Auth. We do not collect your name unless you choose to provide it.

Usage and Wallet Data: We process information associated with your use of SeaVie, including:

  • Documents and document metadata in your digital wallet
  • Name, Surname, Rank, Age, Professional and Social Statuses where you provide or extract them
  • Subscription plan and quota status
  • App language preference

Content You Submit:

  • Scanned document images — when you use the AI document scanner, the image is sent from the app to the SeaVie backend for processing and may then be transmitted to the OpenAI API for vision-based OCR and structured data extraction. The backend implementation also performs image preprocessing before the OpenAI request.
  • PDF documents — documents submitted for AI analysis may be sent by the SeaVie backend to the Anthropic Claude API for processing.

Local Document Storage: Documents and their associated images may be stored locally on your device as part of your SeaVie digital wallet. This local storage is separate from temporary transmission of images to our backend and third-party AI providers for processing.

Payment Information: Subscription payments are processed through the Apple App Store. We do not collect or store credit card information. RevenueCat is used to manage subscription entitlements.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide digital wallet functionality for maritime documents
  • To provide AI-powered document OCR, recognition, extraction, and analysis
  • To provide maritime inspection assistance and related AI features
  • To manage your account, subscription, and usage quota
  • To improve the accuracy and relevance of responses and extracted information
  • To authenticate your identity and prevent unauthorized access
  • To send important service notifications (not marketing)

3. Third-Party Services

SeaVie uses the following third-party services to deliver its functionality:

  • Supabase — authentication and database services. Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Claude API — AI processing of submitted text, images, and documents for supported features. Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI API — vision-based processing of scanned document images for OCR and structured data extraction. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription and purchase entitlement management. Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store — app distribution and payment processing. Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Security

Your account and server-side application data are stored using Supabase infrastructure. We implement security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for data transmission
  • JWT token-based authentication
  • Row-level security policies where applicable
  • Access controls for backend services

Documents and images in your digital wallet may remain stored locally on your device. AI processing involves transmitting submitted content to the SeaVie backend and, where applicable, third-party AI providers such as OpenAI or Anthropic. We do not represent that all content transmitted to third-party providers is permanently stored or immediately deleted; their handling is governed by their respective terms and policies.

5. Data Retention

  • Account data — retained while your account is active and as necessary for legitimate business or legal purposes
  • Wallet documents and images — may remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app, subject to your device's storage and backup settings
  • AI processing data — submitted images and documents are transmitted for processing; retention by third-party AI providers is subject to their applicable policies and service terms
  • Chat history — currently stored on your device where supported by the app

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and associated personal data, subject to legal requirements
  • Portability — receive applicable personal data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection or restriction — object to or request restriction of certain processing where provided by applicable law

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@dse.codes.

7. Children's Privacy

SeaVie is designed for maritime professionals and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. Enterprise and Fleet Accounts

If you use SeaVie through an Enterprise or Fleet account provided by your employer:

  • Your employer (Fleet Admin) may receive usage statistics or other information made available through the enterprise service
  • Company documents uploaded by Fleet Admins may be used to provide contextual answers
  • Individual questions are not intentionally shared with your employer unless the enterprise service or applicable agreement provides otherwise

9. International Data Transfers

SeaVie is operated from Ukraine. Your data may be processed in countries where SeaVie or its service providers operate, including the United States and European Union. Where required by applicable law, we seek to use appropriate safeguards for international data transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app, website, or email where appropriate. The updated policy will state its effective date.
