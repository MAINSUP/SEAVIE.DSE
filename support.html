Frequently Asked Questions

How many documents can I add to wallet?
At the moment, there is no limitation
Can I upload documents?
Yes — tap the add document button to perfom image scan with your phone.
How do I cancel my subscription?
General procedure: Go to iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → SeaVia → Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of the billing period.
Is my data safe?
Yes. Images are processed in real-time and never stored. Your account data is encrypted. See our Privacy Policy for details.
